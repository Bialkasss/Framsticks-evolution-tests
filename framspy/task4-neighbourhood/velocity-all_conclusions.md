# Velocity landscape conclusions

The common plot scale is 0 to 0.0176068505927, the best sampled velocity across all representations.

The neighborhood scatter plots show how mutant fitness is distributed around collected parents. Points above the y=x line improve on their parent; points below it are worse. The better-mutant fractions are:

- f0: 8.02% (320/3991 feasible mutants)
- f1: 1.95% (78/3991 feasible mutants)
- f4: 2.01% (80/3972 feasible mutants)
- fH: 3.45% (137/3974 feasible mutants)

A representation with a larger fraction above y=x offers more improving one-mutation directions near the sampled solutions. A concentration below the line indicates local resistance or that the climb has reached high-quality local optima. Wider vertical spread indicates a more rugged or heterogeneous local landscape. These conclusions describe the sampled neighborhoods, not the complete genotype spaces.
